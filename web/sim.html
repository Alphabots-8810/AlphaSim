Field calibration

The robot multiplies its flywheel speed by kSpeed. It is the one number you tune at an event.

What if the model is wrong

The aim assumes drag 0.50, no spin and a 0.50 m exit. Change what really happens, watch the balls drift, then fix it with kSpeed.
