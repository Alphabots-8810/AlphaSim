Distance in, aim out.

AlphaSim works out the launch angle and flywheel speed that give an FRC 2026 REBUILT shooter the best chance of scoring from 1.5 to 5 m. The answer ships as three short formulas for robot code, with one number, kSpeed, to tune at the field.

Open the simulator Read the report

Try a distance

The simulator solves the shot live, fires a 100-ball burst, and shows where each ball lands and why that angle was chosen.

See the reasoning

The report covers the flight model, the error budget, and the results: about 99.7 % of balls score from 3 m and 96.3 % from 5 m with the scatter we assume.

Put it on a robot

The handoff folder has ShooterAim.java, a Python twin and the coefficients as JSON, generated for a 0.50 m exit height.
